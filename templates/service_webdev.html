How it works

We design and build clean, fast, professional websites for individuals and businesses — the kind of site that presents your work or company properly online. Every project follows the same process below, from discovery through to handover.

  1. 1

    Discovery call

    We go over what the site needs to do, who it's for, and gather your content, branding and any pages you already have in mind.

  2. 2

    Structure & wireframe

    We map out the site's pages and layout so the structure is agreed before any visual design begins.

  3. 3

    Design

    A custom visual design is created for your site — colours, typography and layout suited to your brand, not a generic template.

  4. 4

    Development

    The site is built to be fast, responsive on every device, and easy to navigate.

  5. 5

    Review & revisions

    You review the live preview and we make revisions until you're happy with the result.

  6. 6

    Handover

    The finished site is deployed and handed over to you, along with a short walkthrough of how it works.
