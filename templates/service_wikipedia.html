How it works

Thank you for your interest in our Wikipedia editing service. Every article we take on goes through the same structured process, from an honest notability check through to publication and initial post-publication support — so you always know exactly where things stand.

  1. 1

    Notability assessment

    We review the available independent, reliable sources to honestly assess whether the subject meets Wikipedia's notability guidelines before any work begins.

  2. 2

    Research & source verification

    Our editors gather and verify secondary sources — press coverage, publications and citations — that will support every claim in the article.

  3. 3

    Article preparation

    The article is drafted in a neutral, encyclopedic tone that follows Wikipedia's manual of style and formatting conventions.

  4. 4

    Revisions

    You review the draft and we refine it based on your feedback before it goes anywhere near publication.

  5. 5

    Publication assistance

    We guide the article through submission and publication, responding to any reviewer queries along the way.

  6. 6

    Post-publication support

    We monitor the article after it goes live and handle initial edits or reviewer follow-ups.
